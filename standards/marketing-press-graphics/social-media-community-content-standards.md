# Px Social Media & Community Content Standards

**Guidelines for X Posts, Community Updates, Educational Content, and Public Communications**

*Part of the Pixel Journey Standards Codex — Marketing, Press & Graphics Pillar*

---

## Purpose

Much of Pixel Journey’s public presence happens through X (Twitter), Discord/community channels, GitBook updates, and educational content. Maintaining consistent quality, tone, and educational value in these channels is important for brand reputation and community trust.

This document provides standards for social media and community-facing content.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Educational Value First** — Most public content should teach something or provide genuine value, even when it’s hype or celebration.
2. **On-Brand Voice** — Follow the Brand Voice & Messaging Standards (confident, approachable, slightly playful, community-oriented).
3. **Visual Consistency** — All graphics and images should follow the Visual Asset & Graphics Standards.
4. **Transparency & Honesty** — Be clear about current status, progress, and limitations. Avoid over-promising.
5. **Community Respect** — Celebrate holders, builders, and contributors. Avoid insider-only language that excludes newcomers.

---

## X (Twitter) Content Standards

### Types of Posts

- **Educational / Thread** — High priority. Explain concepts, patterns, or decisions in an accessible way.
- **Progress / Update** — Share meaningful milestones with context.
- **Hype / Celebration** — Okay in moderation. Should still feel premium and on-brand.
- **Community Highlights** — Spotlight holders, builders, or interesting community creations.
- **Memes** — Welcome when they’re high-quality and on-brand. Follow visual asset standards.

### Best Practices

- Lead with value or a clear hook.
- Use threads for complex topics.
- Include relevant visuals or diagrams when they add clarity.
- Tag relevant accounts thoughtfully (not spammy).
- Use consistent hashtags or naming conventions when appropriate.
- Avoid excessive hype language without substance.

---

## Educational Content Standards

Educational content (threads, GitBook updates, docs, videos, etc.) should:

- Be accurate and well-researched.
- Explain the “why” in addition to the “how”.
- Use clear structure (headings, bullet points, examples).
- Link to relevant standards or deeper resources when helpful.
- Be accessible to people with different levels of Web3 experience.

---

## Community Channel Standards (Discord, etc.)

- Maintain the same brand voice as public posts.
- Be helpful and welcoming to newcomers.
- Encourage high-signal discussion rather than low-effort spam.
- Use threads and channels appropriately for different types of conversation.
- Moderate with consistency and respect.

---

## Quality Bar

AAA-tier social media and community content in Px means:
- Content feels thoughtful, consistent, and on-brand
- Educational posts genuinely help people learn
- Visuals and copy work well together
- The community feels respected and included
- The project presents a professional yet characterful public presence

---

## How This Connects to Other Standards

- **Brand Voice & Messaging Standards**: All written content must align with the defined voice.
- **Visual Asset & Graphics Standards**: All images and graphics must meet these standards.
- **AI & Generated Content Guidelines**: AI-assisted social or community content must be refined to meet quality bar.
