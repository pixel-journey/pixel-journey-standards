# Px Standards Roadmap & Future Evolution

**Long-Term Direction and Evolution of the Pixel Journey Standards Codex**

*Part of the Pixel Journey Standards Codex*

---

## Purpose

This document outlines the current state of the standards codex and its intended future evolution. It helps maintainers and contributors understand the direction and priorities for ongoing improvement.

---

## Current State (v0.9+)

As of mid-2026, the codex has reached a mature and comprehensive state with strong coverage in:

- Strategy and core principles (Existing Public Primitives First)
- Concrete implementation patterns
- Very deep UI/UX standards (visual language, components, motion, forms, states, accessibility, data visualization, responsive design)
- Marketing, Press & Graphics standards
- Scaffolds & Boilerplates alignment with the templates repository
- Quality gates, daily tools, and AI-assisted development guidance
- Education and ecosystem integration

The codex is now actively usable for both Px development work and template creation.

---

## Near-Term Priorities (Next 3–6 Months)

- Continue gathering real-world feedback from application on Px repos and templates
- Refine documents based on usage (especially UI/UX and scaffolds sections)
- Strengthen cross-references and navigation as the codex grows
- Improve AI agent support and prompt alignment with the templates repo
- Expand the Marketing/Press/Graphics pillar if new needs emerge

---

## Medium-Term Directions

- Develop more companion practical guides (similar to how templates repo has focused guides)
- Consider adding standards for testing, observability, and performance in production dApps
- Explore standards for community governance and contribution processes
- Potentially create a lightweight "Standards Certification" or review checklist for major Px releases

---

## Long-Term Vision

The codex should remain a living document that evolves with the Pixel Journey ecosystem. It should continue to:

- Raise the quality bar across engineering, design, and communication
- Serve as the connective tissue between different Px repositories
- Support both human developers and AI-assisted development effectively
- Stay pragmatic and actionable rather than becoming overly academic

---

## How to Contribute to Evolution

- Use the `standards-proposal` issue template for new ideas or improvements
- Propose new patterns discovered during real work
- Suggest refinements when documents feel unclear or incomplete in practice
- Help keep the codex aligned with the templates repo and other Px projects

---

*This roadmap is intentionally lightweight and will evolve based on real needs.*
