# Education & Onboarding Pillar

**Helping Humans and AI Agents Rapidly Become Effective Px Contributors**

*Part of the Pixel Journey Standards Codex*

---

## Purpose of This Pillar

One of our core values is **educational spirit**. We want every piece of work in the Pixel Journey ecosystem to not only deliver great functionality, but also to teach others (and our future selves) how to build high-quality Web3 experiences on WAX and Antelope.

This pillar exists to:
- Accelerate onboarding for new human contributors and AI agents
- Create living reference material that explains both the "how" and the "why"
- Connect the technical standards in this codex to the broader WAX ecosystem knowledge
- Produce content that can be reused in GitBook, X posts, docs.pixeljourney.xyz, and internal wikis

---

## Current Documents

- `wax-for-px-devs.md` — Tailored introduction to the WAX/Antelope concepts most relevant to current Px work (AtomicAssets, Hyperion, WharfKit, verifiable entropy, client-side patterns, etc.)

More documents will be added as the pillar expands (e.g. "How the Px Standards connect to Design System, Templates, and Blueprint Catalog", deep-dives on specific patterns, contributing guides, etc.).

---

## How This Pillar Connects to the Rest of the Codex

- **Repo Readiness Scorecard**: Educational Spirit is a scored category. Good education-onboarding content helps repos score higher.
- **Every Folder README Rule**: Package and folder READMEs should link to relevant education documents when they introduce important concepts.
- **Web3-OnChain Patterns**: The education pillar amplifies and explains the patterns in `verifiable-onchain-entropy-patterns.md` and `existing-primitives-first.md`.
- **Monorepo & UI/UX Rules**: Onboarding material should reinforce why we have these structural and visual rules.
