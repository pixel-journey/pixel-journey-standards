# Contributing to the Px Standards

**How to Propose Improvements, New Patterns, or Rule Changes**

*Part of the Pixel Journey Standards Codex*

---

## Why Contribute?

The standards exist to raise quality and educational value across all Px work. They improve through real usage and thoughtful contributions.

We welcome contributions that:
- Clarify existing guidance
- Add practical, high-value patterns
- Improve templates or tools
- Identify gaps based on real Px development experience
- Make the codex more actionable for humans and AI agents

---

## How to Contribute

1. **Check existing documents** first to avoid duplication.
2. **Open an issue** using the `standards-proposal` template (highly recommended for larger changes).
3. **Make focused changes** — one document or section at a time when possible.
4. **Follow the spirit** of the existing standards (educational, practical, aligned with our principles).
5. **Submit a Pull Request** with a clear description of the improvement and why it helps.

## What Makes a Good Contribution?

- Increases clarity or actionability
- Adds concrete examples or patterns from real Px work
- Improves educational value
- Helps humans or AI agents produce higher-quality output
- Maintains consistency with the overall codex tone and structure

## What to Avoid

- Overly broad or vague proposals
- Changes that conflict with the Core Principles without strong justification
- Duplicating content that already exists in a clearer form

## Review Process

- Small improvements (typos, clarifications, small examples) can be merged quickly.
- Larger changes or new patterns/rules will be reviewed for alignment with the overall vision and current Px work needs.
