# Px Press Kit & Media Standards

**Official Guidelines for Press Materials, Media Assets, and External Communications**

*Part of the Pixel Journey Standards Codex — Marketing, Press & Graphics Pillar*

---

## Purpose

As Pixel Journey grows, we will increasingly interact with media, journalists, content creators, partners, and the broader Web3 community. Having clear, professional, and on-brand press materials and media standards ensures we present a consistent, high-quality image externally.

This document defines what belongs in official press kits, how media assets should be prepared and used, and general guidelines for external communications.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Professional & On-Brand** — Press materials should feel premium and aligned with the retro pixel + premium glassmorphic identity.
2. **Clear & Accessible** — Journalists and media should be able to quickly understand who we are and what we offer.
3. **Consistent** — All official materials should use approved assets, language, and formatting.
4. **Up-to-Date** — Press kits and media assets should be regularly reviewed and updated as the project evolves.
5. **Respectful of Media** — We provide clear, honest, and useful information without excessive hype.

---

## What Belongs in an Official Press Kit

A standard Px press kit should include:

- **About Pixel Journey** — Short and long versions of the project description (following Brand Voice standards).
- **Key Facts** — One-pager with founding date, blockchain (WAX), core products (PxWallet, Px Hot or Not, PxPackages, PFPs, etc.), and unique value proposition.
- **High-Quality Visual Assets**:
  - Official logos (primary, horizontal, icon-only) in multiple formats (PNG, SVG, with and without background).
  - Brand marks and approved variations.
  - Key art / hero visuals.
  - PFP examples (if relevant for media).
  - Screenshots of main dApps and features (high resolution, clean).
- **Team / Core Contributors** — Short bios and photos (if publicly shared).
- **Contact Information** — Official press / media email and X handle.
- **Links** — Official website, GitHub, docs, X, Discord/community links.

Optional but recommended:
- Recent news / milestone highlights
- Quote from core team (pre-approved)
- One-pager PDF version of the kit

---

## Media Asset Guidelines

- All assets must follow the **Visual Asset & Graphics Standards**.
- Use official color palette and typography treatments.
- Provide assets in multiple resolutions and formats suitable for web, print, and social.
- Include transparent background versions where appropriate.
- Avoid low-resolution or heavily compressed files.
- Clearly label files (e.g., `px-logo-primary.png`, `px-hero-v2-1920x1080.jpg`).

---

## Usage Guidelines

- Media and partners should be encouraged to use official assets rather than creating their own.
- Clear instructions on how to credit Pixel Journey and link back to official sources.
- Prohibited uses (e.g., modifying logos in ways that distort the brand, using assets for unrelated projects).
- When in doubt, direct media to the official press contact.

---

## External Communications Tone

When speaking to media or external partners:
- Follow the **Brand Voice & Messaging Standards** (confident, approachable, educational, slightly playful when appropriate).
- Be honest about current stage and roadmap.
- Avoid over-promising or excessive hype.
- Emphasize the educational and community aspects of Pixel Journey.
- Highlight technical strengths (verifiable mechanics, self-custody focus, WAX infrastructure) when relevant.

---

## Maintenance

- Press kits and media assets should be reviewed at least quarterly or after major releases.
- A designated person or small team should own keeping the kit current.
- Old versions should be archived rather than left publicly available.

---

## Quality Bar

AAA-tier press and media materials in Px means:
- Everything looks and reads professional while staying true to the retro pixel + premium identity.
- Media can quickly understand the project and get the assets they need.
- Communications are clear, honest, and on-brand.
- The project presents a cohesive and trustworthy image externally.

---

## How This Connects to Other Standards

- **Visual Asset & Graphics Standards**: All visual materials must meet these standards.
- **Brand Voice & Messaging Standards**: All written content in press kits and media communications must follow the voice guidelines.
