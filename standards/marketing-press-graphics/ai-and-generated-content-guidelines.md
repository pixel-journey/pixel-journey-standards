# Px AI & Generated Content Guidelines

**Responsible and On-Brand Use of AI Tools for Graphics, Copy, and Content Creation**

*Part of the Pixel Journey Standards Codex — Marketing, Press & Graphics Pillar*

---

## Purpose

AI tools (image generators, writing assistants, code assistants, etc.) are powerful and increasingly used across development, content creation, and marketing. However, raw AI output often lacks the specific Pixel Journey aesthetic, voice, and quality bar.

This document provides guidelines for responsibly and effectively using AI tools while maintaining brand consistency and high standards.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Human Oversight is Mandatory** — AI is a tool, not a replacement for human judgment, taste, and brand stewardship.
2. **Quality Over Speed** — We would rather produce fewer, higher-quality assets than many mediocre ones.
3. **On-Brand Refinement** — Raw AI output must be heavily curated, edited, or post-processed to match Px standards.
4. **Transparency Where Appropriate** — For internal work, heavy AI use is acceptable. For public-facing content, significant AI generation should be disclosed if it meaningfully affects authenticity.
5. **Respect for the Aesthetic** — AI outputs must be refined to align with the retro pixel + premium glassmorphic identity and Brand Voice.

---

## Guidelines by Content Type

### AI-Generated Graphics & Illustrations

- Raw AI outputs should almost never be published directly.
- Always refine, composite, or heavily edit AI-generated images to match the Px visual language (color palette, pixel treatment, composition, lighting).
- Use AI as a starting point or ideation tool, then apply human craft (pixel art techniques, Design System treatments, etc.).
- For memes and social content, AI can help with concepts, but final output should feel intentional and on-brand.
- Avoid publishing generic AI art styles that clash with the retro pixel aesthetic.

**Recommended workflow**:
1. Use AI for rapid ideation and concept exploration.
2. Select promising outputs.
3. Refine heavily in Photoshop, Aseprite, or similar tools to match Px standards.
4. Get review from someone familiar with the visual language guidelines.
5. Only publish when it meets the Visual Asset & Graphics Standards.

### AI-Assisted Writing & Copy

- AI can help with drafting, structure, and ideation.
- Final copy must be reviewed and edited to match the **Brand Voice & Messaging Standards**.
- Avoid generic AI-sounding language (overly polished, repetitive phrasing, lack of personality).
- Inject Px-specific voice, retro references, and community tone during editing.
- Error messages, UI copy, and educational content should receive extra human attention.

### AI for Code & Development

- AI is already heavily used and encouraged for planning, code generation, and refactoring (see AI Agent Workflow Guide).
- All AI-generated code must still pass the **Repo Readiness Scorecard** and follow relevant patterns and rules.
- Human review and testing remain mandatory for production code.

---

## Quality Bar

AAA-tier use of AI in Px means:
- AI is used as a powerful accelerator, not a crutch.
- Final outputs (visual or written) meet or exceed the quality bar defined in the relevant standards.
- The retro pixel + premium glassmorphic identity and Brand Voice are clearly present.
- Human taste and judgment are clearly visible in the final result.
- We move fast without sacrificing quality or brand integrity.

---

## How This Connects to Other Standards

- **Visual Asset & Graphics Standards**: All AI-generated visuals must ultimately meet these standards.
- **Brand Voice & Messaging Standards**: All AI-assisted copy must be refined to match the voice.
- **UI/UX Design Pillar**: In-app copy and visuals generated or assisted by AI must align with these standards.
