# Px Visual Asset & Graphics Standards

**Consistent, On-Brand Standards for Graphics, Illustrations, Memes, and Visual Assets Across Pixel Journey**

*Part of the Pixel Journey Standards Codex — Marketing, Press & Graphics Pillar*

---

## Purpose

Pixel Journey has a strong, distinctive visual identity (retro pixel + premium glassmorphic). All graphics, illustrations, memes, social media assets, press materials, and generated visuals should consistently reinforce this identity while maintaining high quality.

This document sets the baseline standards for visual assets and graphics generation across the ecosystem.

---

## Core Principles

1. **On-Brand Consistency** — Every visual asset should clearly feel like it belongs to Pixel Journey.
2. **High Quality & Intentional** — Assets should look premium, not rushed or low-effort.
3. **Retro Pixel + Modern Premium Balance** — Maintain the core aesthetic while leveraging modern tools and techniques.
4. **Reusability & Modularity** — Where possible, create assets that can be reused or adapted across contexts (social, press, in-app, merch, etc.).
5. **Accessibility & Readability** — Text in graphics should be legible, and important information should not rely on color alone.
6. **Scalability** — Assets should work well across different sizes and contexts (thumbnails, banners, print, etc.).

---

## Visual Style Guidelines

### Core Aesthetic
- Retro pixel art style combined with premium glassmorphic / modern UI treatments where appropriate.
- Use of the official color palette and typography treatments from the Design System.
- Subtle CRT, scanline, or phosphor effects can be used tastefully (especially for terminal/game-like assets).
- Clean composition with strong visual hierarchy.

### What to Avoid
- Generic Web3 gradients or "crypto bro" aesthetics.
- Low-resolution or heavily compressed pixel art that looks cheap.
- Mixing too many visual styles in one asset.
- Overly busy or cluttered compositions.
- Using unrelated meme templates that clash with the Px identity.

---

## Asset Types & Standards

### Social Media Graphics & Memes
- Maintain consistent branding (logo placement, color treatment, typography).
- Educational or hype-focused memes should still feel on-brand and high-quality.
- Use approved templates where available to maintain consistency.
- Text should be highly legible even at small sizes (thumbnails).

### Illustrations & Key Art
- Should reinforce the retro pixel + premium glassmorphic identity.
- Character designs, environments, and key visuals should feel cohesive with in-app UI and PFPs.
- Use consistent lighting, shading, and pixel treatment styles.

### Press & Media Assets
- Official logos, brand marks, and key visuals should follow approved usage guidelines.
- Press kits should include high-quality versions in multiple formats and sizes.
- Screenshots and in-app captures should use consistent framing and aesthetic treatments.

### In-App & UI-Related Graphics
- Any custom illustrations or icons used inside dApps should align with the Design System and component standards.
- Loading screens, empty state illustrations, and onboarding visuals should follow the UI/UX standards.

### Generated Assets (AI or Procedural)
- When using AI image generation or procedural tools, results must be heavily curated and refined to match the Px aesthetic.
- Do not publish raw AI outputs that look off-brand or low-quality.
- Establish clear review processes for generated assets before public use.

---

## Quality Bar

AAA-tier visual assets in Px means:
- Every graphic clearly feels like it belongs to Pixel Journey
- Quality is consistently high across social, press, in-app, and marketing use cases
- The retro pixel + premium identity is reinforced without becoming repetitive or boring
- Assets are intentional, legible, and professional
- Generated or AI-assisted assets are refined to meet the same standard as hand-crafted ones
